## Dream landscape revision

The original loop corridor was replaced after visitor-camera review found that its repeated rings overwhelmed the story and read as visual noise. The revised generated plate keeps the traveler and dusk palette, but opens into a reflective causeway through layered mountains and water. Three distant silhouettes—a carved sandstone courtyard, a timber pavilion, and a stone keep—give the route architectural variety without filling the foreground with low-detail props. The image is a generated art plate, not sourced architectural photography or a claim of cultural authenticity.

A small CC0 Kenney watchtower was inspected during the final pass but excluded: its low-poly game-kit style clashed with the cinematic landscape and would have added another disconnected prop.
